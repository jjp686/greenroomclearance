Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Jamie Wright trading as Purbeck Digital ("Green Room Clearance", "we", "us") uses your personal data when you use greenroomclearance.com or contact us. We are the data controller.

Who we are

Green Room Clearance clears and resells surplus office, AV and IT equipment for businesses across Bournemouth, Christchurch, Poole and the wider Dorset area. Green Room Clearance is part of Purbeck Digital.

Contact: info@greenroomclearance.com, The Paddocks, Wimborne, BH21 2BZ.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, and optionally your company name and phone number, plus your premises location, the type of clearance you need and the details you give us about the equipment.
  • Emails: anything you send to us directly by email.
  • Clearance work: contact details for the people we deal with on site, and records of the equipment we collect.
  • Technical data: basic server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it and our lawful basis

  • Replying to your enquiry and quoting (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract): to understand what you need, arrange a site visit and give you a quote.
  • Carrying out a clearance (contract): to arrange collection, keep records of the equipment and settle payment.
  • Legal obligations: keeping business and tax records we are required to keep.

We do not sell your details or use them for marketing lists. We will only email you about your enquiry or clearance.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help run the site and our email: our website host and our domain and email provider.
  • Google Fonts, which loads the fonts used on this site. Your browser connects to Google's servers to do this, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • Our professional advisers, such as an accountant, where needed.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where your data is processed outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or the International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum.

Data on equipment we clear

Equipment such as computers, phones and meeting room systems can hold data belonging to you or your staff. Please tell us about any devices that may hold data before collection so we can agree how it will be wiped or destroyed.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work: 12 months after our last contact, then deleted. Records of clearances we carry out: 6 years, to meet tax and accounting rules.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to how we use it, and ask for a copy to move elsewhere. Email info@greenroomclearance.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
